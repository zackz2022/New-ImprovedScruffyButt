Beyond the Hormonal Roller Coaster

Hormone Balance for Women Over 40 is an eight-chapter digital wellness guide introducing readers to topics surrounding women's hormonal health, perimenopause, menopause, and lifestyle habits.

The guide explores themes involving estrogen, progesterone, cortisol, nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress, and personal wellness planning.
