Meet Celeste Hannah

Founder of Scruffy Butt Digital Designs and Computer Science student

I love making things people can use. There's always something new to learn.

I'm Celeste Hannah, a mom of three, a wife to my amazing husband, and a creative person who loves to learn. I've survived abuse and worked hard to overcome addiction. These days, my life is full of legal battles. My husband is facing the possibility of life in prison, and I'm dealing with data breach settlements after my Social Security number was exposed and identity theft turned my life upside down. Through it all, I'm pursuing my college degree and a full-time management position while launching Scruffy Butt Digital Designs. I'm proud of how far I've come and determined to build a more stable future for my family.

How I built Scruffy Butt Digital Designs

Scruffy Butt started with an idea I wanted to make real. I built the website, designed book covers, and created an online bookstore and marketplace for digital guides and custom designs. Seeing my ideas become pages people could actually visit made me proud of how far I'd come. I want the guides I offer, from budgeting to personal growth, to give someone a practical place to start when they're working toward a goal of their own.

What I'm working toward

I'm studying Computer Science and working toward a career in cybersecurity. I want to help people protect their privacy and feel more confident online, especially small business owners who are figuring out technology as they go. As Scruffy Butt grows, I hope to take on more website design projects and create plain-language resources for staying safe online.

What keeps me going

I'm still fighting for the future I pictured when I decided to go back to school. Every class I finish and every project I put out into the world brings me a little closer to the stability and independence I want for my family. If you're rebuilding your life too, I hope seeing what I've built encourages you to give that idea you've been holding onto a real chance.
